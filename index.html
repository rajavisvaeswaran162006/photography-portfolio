About

A little about me

I'm an aspiring photographer with a genuine interest in visual storytelling. I enjoy capturing different perspectives, exploring natural light and experimenting with composition using my smartphone.

Every image here was captured on a mobile phone — no professional equipment, just curiosity and an eye for moments that feel worth remembering. I'm drawn to the interplay of light and shadow, the textures of everyday life, and the quiet beauty in ordinary scenes.

I'm currently looking for an opportunity to gain hands-on experience by assisting a professional photography or creative team. I'm eager to learn, willing to put in the work, and genuinely passionate about the craft.

What I Bring

What I can contribute

I may not have professional experience yet, but I bring dedication, adaptability and a willingness to learn.

  • Assisting on shoots — Setting up equipment, managing props, handling lighting setups and supporting the photographer on location.
  • Post-production support — Basic photo editing, colour correction, file management and organising image libraries.
  • Creative eye — An instinct for composition, framing and natural light developed through consistent personal practice.
  • Content creation — Capturing behind-the-scenes footage, social media content and supplementary visual material.
  • Reliability & adaptability — A quick learner who shows up prepared, takes direction well and stays positive under pressure.
